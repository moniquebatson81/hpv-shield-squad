# Try it before publishing

Open `index.html` as explained in README. You do not need to publish to test.

1. Start a mission. Read the lesson, explore every training card, and answer the questions.
2. Pick a wrong answer once. You should receive kind feedback and be able to choose again.
3. Finish all four missions. You should see 160/160 learning points and a digital shield.
4. Choose “Save my shield.” An SVG image should download; open it in a browser.
5. Choose “Play again.” You should return to mission 1 with 0 points. Refreshing also resets the game.
6. Turn sound on and off. It begins off; on produces a short chime after progress. Your device volume still controls loudness.
7. Use only Tab, Shift+Tab, and Enter/Space. All controls should be reachable with a visible focus outline. The skip link should take you to the main area.
8. Try a phone, tablet, and desktop, or resize the browser. Text and choices should fit without horizontal scrolling.
9. Turn on your device's “reduce motion” setting. The floating shield animation should stop. No challenge requires moving quickly.
10. Open the caregiver section. Check the CDC links and medical claims before release. Adult sources may contain more detail than the children's game.
11. If possible, test with a screen reader. Headings, questions, buttons, feedback, and progress should make sense. This has not yet been tested with a human screen-reader user.
12. Ask a caregiver and child to play together. Are the words clear? Is it fun? Does it take roughly 3–5 minutes? No timing study has been performed yet.
