For parents and caregivers: facts and sources

This learning game introduces cancer prevention. HPV is a common virus that can cause certain cancers later in life. HPV vaccination helps prevent several types of cancer; it does not prevent every cancer or treat an existing infection.

CDC recommends routine HPV vaccination at ages 11–12, and vaccination can begin at age 9. The number and timing of doses depend on age at the first dose and health needs. Ask your child's healthcare professional about their recommended schedule.

Vaccines help the immune system recognize and respond to a germ. This game uses shields and training as learning symbols; protection is not instant or perfect. Learning points are not a measure of medical protection.
